Vibershot 0.1.3

Editor
- New layout: tools are a compact icon grid, with the active tool's settings underneath in groups (Style, Colour, Line, Text, Effects).
- Set the look before you draw: Arrow, Shape, Text and Callout show the style of the next item, so colour and width can be chosen first.
- Library sidebar on the right: ready-made arrows, shapes, callouts and stamps with live previews. Click one to insert it, or drag it onto the picture.
- Themes: pick a look (Default, Vibershot, Signal red, Blueprint, Marker) and every preset and new item uses its colours and line width. Items already on the picture keep their look.
- Layers moved to the right sidebar, under the library.
- The right sidebar folds down to a strip: use the arrow beside "Library" or View > Collapse sidebar. Your choice is remembered.
- With nothing selected, the Select tool shows the picture settings: canvas size, image size and canvas colour.
