Vibershot 0.3.0

Record sound and a webcam (new)
- A recording can now include system audio, a microphone and a webcam. Each is recorded as its own track.
- Before recording starts, choose what to include, pick the microphone and camera, set the microphone volume and watch the live level meters.
- The webcam shows in a floating preview: drag it into place, scroll over it to resize, right-click it for its shape.
- When you stop, open the tracks in the video editor to trim, move the camera or mute a sound, or save everything as one MP4.
- Settings > Video holds the starting choices and a switch to start recording straight away. New shortcuts pause a recording and mute the microphone.

Polish
- The recording window is tidier: aligned switches and meters, and while recording it shows only what is being recorded.
- More icons: recording buttons, the region action bar, the preview window and the Settings navigation.
