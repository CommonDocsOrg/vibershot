Vibershot 0.2.0

Video editor (new)
- Open an MP4 from the gallery to edit it on a layered timeline: trim either end of a clip, split at the playhead or with the razor, and drag clips along the timeline and between layers.
- Stack videos and pictures (PNG, JPEG, BMP, WebP) over one another. Move, resize, turn and crop each one right in the preview, and set how see-through it is.
- Audio lanes show a waveform. A video's sound can be detached and edited on its own.
- Elements tab: text, titles, lower thirds, callouts, shapes, arrows and stamps. Drag one onto the picture or the timeline, double-click text to type in place, and restyle it in the panel.
- Captions: press T to add one at the playhead on its own track. Import and export .srt files; captions are drawn into the exported video.
- Projects save as .vibevideo files that link to your original MP4s.
- Export MP4 renders the result at up to 4K.
- Large files preview from low-resolution proxies so scrubbing stays quick; the export always uses the originals.
